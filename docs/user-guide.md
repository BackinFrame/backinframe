# User guide — receiving memories

*For the person who gets the photos.*

## What is this?

Someone set up Back in Frame for you. Every now and then you get a notification on your phone with a
photo or short video, plus when it was taken and how long ago that was.

You receive them in one of these ways. The person who set this up chooses one with you:
the Back in Frame web app, the ntfy app, e-mail, Telegram, Signal, Matrix or Discord. Captions,
buttons and replies come in your own language if they set it for you.

## With the Back in Frame web app

You get a personal link. Nothing to install from an app store, no account.

1. **Open the link** on your phone.
2. **iPhone or iPad:** the page shows the steps for your phone, with a small animation you can
   pause or step through. In short:
   - **iOS 26:** tap **•••** next to the address bar, then **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**;
     leave *Open as Web App* on and tap **Add**.
   - **iOS 16.4 to 18:** tap the Share button (the square with the arrow), then **Add to Home
     Screen**, then **Add**.
   - **Opened inside WhatsApp, Instagram or another app?** Open the link in Safari first. The page
     has a *Copy link* button for that.
   - **Older than iOS 16.4?** Notifications cannot work; you can still look at your memories.

   Then open Back in Frame from your home screen. Apple only allows notifications from there.
3. **Tap Turn on notifications** and allow them.

The app shows everything you received, newest first. Tap a photo to see it larger.

Tap the **heart** under a photo you like. That photo then comes back a bit more often. The person
who sends you the memories can see which photos got a heart; tap the heart again to take it back.

In the first two weeks of January you get a **year review**: a page with a selection of the
memories you received the year before. The sender can turn this off for you.

## With the ntfy app

1. **Install ntfy.** It is free, has no ads and needs no account:
   - Android: *ntfy* on Google Play or F-Droid
   - iPhone/iPad: *ntfy* in the App Store
2. **Subscribe.** You get a QR code, or a server and a *topic* (the long name that starts with
   `backinframe-`).
   - **Android:** scan the QR code with your camera. The ntfy app opens and subscribes you
     right away, to the right server.
   - **iPhone, or by hand:** in the ntfy app, tap **+** and enter the topic. If the server is
     not `ntfy.sh`, first turn on *Use another server* and enter that address.
3. **Allow notifications** when the app asks.

You get a welcome message right away.

## By e-mail

Nothing to do: the photo arrives as an e-mail with the photo in it. Videos are attached. To
change how often, ask the person who sends them.

## With Telegram

1. Open the bot the sender tells you about (a link like `t.me/…`) and tap **Start**.
2. The bot answers with your **chat ID**, a number. Send that number to the person who set this
   up.
3. Photos arrive in that chat, with **More**, **Less** and **Pause 1 week** buttons under each.

## With Signal

Photos arrive as Signal messages from a number the sender gives you; save it as a contact
(for example "Back in Frame"). Reply **more**, **less**, **pause** or **resume** to change the
rhythm. Words in your own language work too, like *vaker*, *mehr*, *plus* or *más*.

## With Matrix or Discord

The sender posts into a room or channel you share. Everyone in that room or channel sees the
photos. There are no buttons; ask the sender to change how often.

## How often

The first photo arrives at a random moment within your time window, which is 09:00–21:00 unless
agreed otherwise. Set your own rhythm with the buttons (in the web app, under every photo in
ntfy and Telegram) or, on Signal and Telegram, by replying with the word:

| Button | What happens |
|---|---|
| **More** | More photos. Steps: 1 every 3 days → 1 every 2 days → 1 a day → 2 a day → … |
| **Less** | Fewer photos, in the same steps the other way round |
| **Pause 1 week** | No photos for 7 days. **Resume** or **More** ends the pause early. |

## Tips

- **Saving a photo:** open it and use your phone's share or save button.
- **ntfy:** photos disappear from the app after a while (on ntfy.sh, a few hours). Save the ones
  you want to keep. The web app keeps your last 60.
- **iPhone, no notifications?** Web app: open it from the home screen, not from Safari. ntfy:
  check *Settings → Notifications → ntfy*.
- **No “Add to Home Screen” in the list?** Scroll to the bottom of the share list, tap *Edit
  Actions* and add it. Or open the page in Safari first.
- **Screen reader, large text or keyboard?** The web app works with VoiceOver and TalkBack, with
  larger text and with a keyboard, and the animation stands still if you turned on *Reduce
  Motion*.
- **Want to stop?** Web app: tap *Turn off on this device*, or remove it from your home screen.
  ntfy: unsubscribe from the topic. Or ask the person who set it up to remove you.

## Got an upload link?

Someone who sends memories can give you a link to add your own photos to one of their albums: the
grandchildren from your side of the family, your pictures of that weekend. Open the link on your
phone, choose photos or videos, and tap *Add*. You can only add: you cannot see or delete anything
there. Your name is shown as "from …" with your photos.

## Privacy

- Your link (or topic name) is your private key to the photos. Do not share it.
- With e-mail, Telegram, Signal, Matrix or Discord the sender knows that one address of yours
  (your e-mail address, chat ID, phone number or room), and the photo passes through that
  service under its own privacy terms. With the web app and ntfy they know none of these.
- Back in Frame collects nothing about you: no account, no phone number, no tracking. All it
  receives from your phone is a button tap and, for the web app, the address your phone uses for
  notifications.
- Web app notifications are end-to-end encrypted: Apple, Google or Mozilla deliver them but cannot
  read them. Photos come without location data.
